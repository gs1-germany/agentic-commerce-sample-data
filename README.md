# Agentic Commerce Sample Data

Experimentation repo for hosting **machine-interpretable product master data** in the context of Agentic Commerce, where AI agents find, compare and purchase products on behalf of consumers or businesses.

## Purpose

A learning-by-doing pilot: gather experience, iterate, and identify what already works well — and where gaps remain — so companies can position themselves for Agentic Commerce.
